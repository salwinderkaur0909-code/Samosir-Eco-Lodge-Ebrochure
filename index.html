Vocabulary Notes

Key cultural and ecological terminology used in the project

1. Horas

Greeting / Cultural Value

A traditional Batak greeting expressing warmth, blessing, peace, and good health to visitors upon arrival.

2. Ruma Bolon

Indigenous Architecture

Traditional Batak stilt house featuring curved roofs and natural wood ventilation systems that eliminate artificial cooling needs.

3. Marsialap Ari

Social / Eco-Philosophy

A traditional Batak value of mutual cooperation applied in community-driven eco-hospitality and organic farming.

4. Ulos

Sustainable Textile

Sacred handwoven Batak textile symbolizing respect and warmth, made using natural plant dyes and eco-crafting methods.

5. Zero-Waste Hospitality

Ecological Practice

An eco-friendly lodging framework focusing on elimination of single-use plastics, active composting, and utilizing natural organic tableware like bamboo cups.
