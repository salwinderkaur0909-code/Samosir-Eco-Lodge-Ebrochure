Cultural Harmony & Sustainability

A Sustainable Sanctuary Built on Tradition

Nestled along the serene shores of Lake Toba, our lodge is built using authentic Ruma Bolon architectural concepts. These traditional Batak stilt houses naturally maximize cross-ventilation, keeping your rooms cool and peaceful while reducing energy usage.

We strictly adhere to Marsialap Ari—a cherished Batak philosophy of mutual community cooperation. By working alongside local villagers, we run organic gardens, offer zero-waste dining, and protect the pristine environment of Samosir Island.
